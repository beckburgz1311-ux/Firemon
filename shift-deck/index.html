How to play

Workers are your active battlers. They use Composure instead of HP and take Stress instead of damage.

Customers are one-use effect cards. They can heal staff, create chaos, drain Shift Points or pressure the rival venue.

At the start of each turn, your maximum Shift Points increase by one, up to five. Moves spend Shift Points.

You may roster one Worker and handle one Customer per turn. Your active Worker can then use one move, which ends your turn.

When a Worker reaches zero Composure, they are Sent on Break. The opponent gains one Tip. Collect three Tips to win the shift.
