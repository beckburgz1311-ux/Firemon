# Shift Deck

A mobile-first retro card battle game inspired by handheld card RPGs, rebuilt around hospitality workers and customers.

## Core rules

- Worker cards are active battlers.
- Composure replaces HP.
- Stress replaces damage.
- Shift Points pay for moves.
- Customer cards create one-use effects.
- Workers reduced to zero Composure are Sent on Break.
- Each Worker sent on break awards one Tip.
- First venue to collect three Tips wins.

- Mobile-first card hand and controls
- Player versus AI battle
- Active Worker and two-card bench
- Two moves per Worker
- Customer effect cards
- Shift Point progression
- Composure, Stress, Guard and status effects
- Tip victory condition
- Local career win/loss record
