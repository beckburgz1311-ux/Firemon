# Firemon

A mobile-first pixel FPS and original monster-catching prototype.

## Current loop

- Explore a low-resolution ray-cast maze.
- Aim and shoot wild Firemon in real time.
- See floating damage numbers when shots land.
- Weaken and stagger Firemon to improve capture odds.
- Enter turn-based duels with Attack, Skill, Guard, Capture and Flee.
- Catch Firemon and swap your active team member.
- Survive melee and projectile attacks outside battle.
- Slowly regenerate HP and ammunition while no enemy is hunting you.
- Save progress locally in the browser.

## Visual upgrades

- Pixel scanline overlay
- Screen shake and red damage flash when hit
- Animated health bars and messages
- Improved battle shadows and sprite presentation
- Polished team cards and controls
- Radar, compass and live player coordinates

## Controls

### Phone

- Left thumb pad: move and strafe
- Drag the game view: aim
- Fire: shoot
- Duel: enter a turn-based battle with the aimed target
- Cap: throw a quick capture capsule at a weakened target
- Team: view and swap captured Firemon

### Keyboard

- WASD: move
- Arrow keys: aim
- Space: fire
- E: duel
- C: quick capture
- T: team
